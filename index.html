    About Data Seeker

    Data Seeker finds public sequencing datasets for a gene or topic, scores them for reliability, and produces a list of the exact files to download.

    How it works

    Search. Your term is sent to NCBI GEO (series records), the ENCODE portal and EBI ArrayExpress at the same time. With alias expansion on, NCBI Gene supplies the gene's official symbol and aliases (MyoD also finds MYOD1, Myod1 and MYF3); short or ambiguous aliases start switched off. ArrayExpress copies of GEO studies are merged into the GEO record.

    Metadata. Species come from the repository; cell lines, cell types and tissues are matched against a dictionary of about 40 names in the title, summary and sample names; the assay comes from the GEO data type and keywords.

    Study design. Each study's sample names are checked for your term to count the samples about your target, and for input, IgG or whole-cell extract controls that share their cell line or condition. A study is dedicated when at least half its samples (or all of a study of four or fewer) are target samples or their controls, partly about the target from 15%, and a minor part below that. Time courses, knockouts, overexpression, drug treatments, disease models, patient cohorts, three or more ChIP targets, three or more cell types and 40 or more samples count as design factors: two make a design moderate, four complex.

    Reliability score (0 to 100). Points for a linked publication (+25), ENCODE processing standards (+35), two or more samples on your target (+15), a matched control (+8), raw reads deposited (+15), processed files (+10), a study dedicated to your target (+10) and release in the last eight years (+5). Points off when the target is a minor part of a bigger study (−15), only mentioned in the text (−5), the design is complex (−10), a ChIP has no control (−5), the record is a SuperSeries (−5), or ENCODE audits flag problems. Tier A is 70 or more, Tier B 45 to 69, Tier C below 45. Each result lists its own breakdown.

    Download list. For each chosen GEO sample the app reads the sample's GEO record for its supplementary files and genome build, and finds its SRA runs through NCBI and FASTQ links through ENA. ENCODE files come from the ENCODE file list with their size, assembly and md5.

    Your data

    Everything runs in this single HTML file. To search, your search terms and the accessions you pick are sent to NCBI, ENCODE and EBI; nothing else leaves the browser and nothing is uploaded. Each browser tab keeps its own workspace (project details, last search, saved datasets and download list) in this browser's storage, and the 20 most recent workspaces are kept. Your NCBI API key, if you add one, is stored once for all tabs. Save workspace keeps a portable copy as a .json file.

    Limitations

    • The score is a triage aid built from metadata, not a judgement of data quality. Check antibodies, controls and replicates on the repository page before relying on a dataset.
    • Cell types, controls and design factors are read from sample names and free text, so unusual naming can be missed or misread.
    • GEO search returns up to 500 series per query; narrow the search if the total is larger.
    • Some browsers block reading GEO sample records directly. The download list then gives each sample's GEO folder instead of individual file names.
    • Genome builds are taken from the text of GEO records and are empty when a study does not state them.
    • Search results are not stored in the workspace; reopening a workspace runs its search again, so counts can change as repositories update.
    • The app needs a network connection to search. Workspaces, saved datasets and download lists open offline.

    Using Data Seeker

    Find public sequencing datasets, judge which to trust, and produce a list of the files to download.

    1 · Search
    Enter a gene or topic, pick the data type and species, and press Search. Results appear below, grouped by reliability tier. Filter on the left, open Details for samples and the score breakdown, and star the datasets to keep. Saved in the header opens your starred datasets in a side panel; they are kept across all searches in this workspace.
    2 · Download list
    Tick the samples and file types you need, then save the file list: one row per file with its URL, plus SRA runs for raw reads.
    3 · About
    Method, scoring rules, authorship and how to cite.

    Toolbar

    Saved opens your starred datasets in a side panel. Download list opens the list in a side panel without leaving your results. Reset clears this workspace. Save / Load workspace stores or restores the workspace as a .json file; Save writes back to the file you last saved or opened where the browser allows. Click the icon for full screen.

    Several tabs

    Each tab works on its own workspace. Earlier workspaces are listed at the foot of the Search tab, under Project and workspaces.

    Reset everything?

    This clears this workspace's search, results, saved datasets, download list and project details. It can't be undone. Save a workspace first if you want to come back to this. Other workspaces and your NCBI API key are kept.
